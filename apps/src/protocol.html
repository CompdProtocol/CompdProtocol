inside boundaries.

COMPD separates Flap revenue intake, Vault accounting and guarded strategy or buyback execution. The off-chain Agent prepares decisions while protocol rules constrain actions.

System topology

Separate intake, custody, accounting and trigger authority.

On-chain guardrails

Policy boundaries for holder access, capital exposure and execution.

HOLDER ACCESS500,000 COMPD · hold-only

Read-only Agent Intelligence; no staking, deposit, lockup or operational authority.

STRATEGY EXPOSURE30% aggregate · 10% per position

At most three concurrent positions; spot-equivalent 1× with no borrowing or margin.

LOSS BOUNDARIES3% designed position loss

A daily realized-loss circuit and equity drawdown guard restrict new exposure.

BUYBACKBounded by reserve and market controls

20% per execution, 0.50 BNB-equivalent hard cap, six-hour cooldown and 40% period cap.

PAUSE CONTROLReversible operations latch

Restricts new exposure, buybacks and exposure-increasing compound activity.

ROLE SEPARATIONExecution is narrowly scoped

Agent preparation, guarded execution, administration and treasury controls remain distinct.
