01 / Overview

What COMPD is designed to do

COMPD is a holder-access strategy framework built around project treasury separation. Holders keep tokens in their own wallets. Agent Intelligence is read-only; it does not grant custody over strategy funds or operational control.

BUILT AROUND CLEAR BOUNDARIES
Holder access, treasury accounting and guarded execution remain separate parts of the system.

02 / Holder access

Hold-only qualification

The V1 threshold is 500,000 COMPD at 18 decimals. Qualification is based on holding the balance; no staking, deposit, lockup, personal vault or user trading balance is required. Holder Intelligence is read-only and may include the current strategy, signal, market, timeframe, entry, target, invalidation, reason summary and position progress.

Holders are passive. Top30 eligible holders with at least500,000 COMPD are ranked by TWAB; no staking, deposits, locking or manual claims. The initial checkpoint is launch+15minutes, followed by regular UTC8-hour windows. The initial onchain payout remains Not Activated. Holder access grants no treasury, strategy, administrative, governance or execution authority.

03 / Accounting

Keep the two flows separate

Bootstrap is10% Strategy Capital /90% Non-Strategy Treasury Principal. Each recurring allocation follows its own economic source. Positive realized Agent profit is routed only after settlement and principal return. Flap protocol revenue follows a separate allocation. Unrealized PnL, returned principal and losses do not enter the profit split.

04 / Buyback policy

Disciplined execution. Bounded by protocol rules.

Buyback Reserve may be used only through a guarded execution path. The requested amount is bounded by the reserve percentage, immutable hard cap and remaining period allowance.

05 / Strategy boundaries

Capital limits are explicit

Approved assets: NVDAx, TSLAx, AMZNx, AAPLx, SPYx. The live Agent evaluates every8hours plus immediate launch evaluation. Live trading is Not Activated pending approved venue, price validation, entry/exit and settlement.

  • Maximum aggregate active capital exposure: 30% of Strategy Capital.
  • Maximum per-position exposure: 10% of Strategy Capital; no more than three concurrent positions.
  • Spot-equivalent 1× only. No borrowing or margin.
  • Daily realized-loss circuit: 5% of Strategy Capital at the start of the UTC day.
  • Drawdown guard: 10% from reconciled equity high-water mark; new entries require Guardian review and explicit resume after a breach.
  • Designed loss for one position may not exceed 3% of trade principal.
  • New exposure and buybacks require an approved market/asset route and verified, sufficiently fresh pricing.

Paper risk metrics are virtual accounting. They do not demonstrate production enforcement or investment performance.

06 / Protocol safeguards

Designed to protect capital under changing conditions.

Protocol-level safeguards restrict execution when defined operating conditions are not met. Strategy execution and treasury operations remain separated, with privileged actions constrained by explicit roles and protocol rules.

PAUSE CONTROL

Temporarily restricts new execution while preserving treasury accounting.

ROLE SEPARATION

Operational roles remain separated across execution, administration and treasury controls.

PROTECTED RECOVERY

Recovery paths are restricted by protocol state and predefined authorization rules.

08 / FAQ

Questions & answers

Do holders need to deposit COMPD?

No. Qualification is hold-only. Tokens stay in the holder’s wallet; no staking, deposit or lockup is required for Agent Intelligence access.

Can COMPD holders direct treasury activity?

No. Holder access is read-only and grants no treasury, strategy, administrative, governance or execution authority.

How does COMPD account for treasury activity?

Strategy Capital, Buyback Reserve and Protocol Reserve are accounted separately under defined rules. Agent profit and Flap revenue use different allocation splits.

How do COMPD buybacks work?

Buybacks use a guarded execution path with predefined reserve thresholds, execution limits, cooldowns, approved routes and market-protection controls.

How does the Agent interact with the treasury?

The Agent prepares strategy decisions. Guarded protocol components enforce execution and treasury boundaries.

What is the paper workspace for?

It observes strategy behavior in an isolated paper environment. It does not interact with production treasury capital, and its results are not production performance.

Does COMPD V1 distribute holder rewards?

No. V1 uses hold-only access to read-only Agent Intelligence; it does not include staking, holder reward distributions or claim custody.
